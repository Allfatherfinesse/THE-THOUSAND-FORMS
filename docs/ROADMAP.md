# Development roadmap

## Phase 0: Design and validation
- Confirm UE5 environment and project ownership.
- Select three starting martial arts and a weapon direction.
- Write combat state machine, animation requirements, and prototype acceptance criteria.
- Establish asset licensing and source-control strategy (Git LFS for binary assets).

## Phase 1: Combat vertical slice
- One small training arena with placeholder art.
- Three distinct playable disciplines.
- Responsive attacks, defenses, hit reactions, targeting and recovery.
- One training dummy/sparring enemy and one boss with readable telegraphs.
- Early hybrid technique loadout and stance transitions.
- Input latency, collision, camera and performance testing.

## Phase 2: Progression
- Mastery challenges, discipline unlocks, skill trees and hybrid build rules.
- Save/load, tuning tools and test coverage.

## Phase 3: World slice
- One explorable monastery region, shortcuts, lore, miniboss, boss reward.
- Visual and audio direction, optimization and accessibility.

## Exit criteria for first prototype
- A clean playable loop, documented controls, no known blocking bugs.
- Measurable input responsiveness and consistent hit detection.
- Styles visibly and mechanically distinguishable.
- Hybrid techniques do not cause animation or combat-state deadlocks.
- Boss has fair, recognizable attack patterns and repeatable QA scenarios.

No phases are marked completed yet.
