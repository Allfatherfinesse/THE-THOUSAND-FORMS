# THE THOUSAND FORMS: Game Design Foundation
Status: concept / pre-production, 2026-11-12.

## Pillars
- Demanding, responsive Soulslike combat where player skill matters.
- Martial arts disciplines serve as starting classes, with distinct move sets, strengths, weaknesses, skill trees and philosophies.
- Bosses and hidden masters unlock disciplines; unlocking is not instant mastery.
- Players can combine learned techniques into custom hybrid fighting styles.
- Mythological dark fantasy with interconnected kingdoms, ancient monasteries, corrupted grandmasters and secrets.
- Some recurring rivals may adapt to observable player habits; behavior must be readable and counterable.

## Fictional premise (working lore)
The First Master sought to unite martial knowledge into the Thousand Forms. A failed transcendence fractured the tradition; disciples founded rival kingdoms and the masters became corrupted. A survivor from a destroyed monastery uncovers the truth and chooses the future of the arts. Names and plot details are provisional.

## Candidate disciplines
Muay Thai: close-range pressure, elbows, knees, clinch.
Wing Chun: deflection, close-range combinations and centerline control.
Taekwondo: long-range kicks, footwork and mobility.
Judo: off-balancing, throws and reversals.
Future candidates: Capoeira, Baguazhang, Karate, Boxing.
Avoid treating these as rigid stereotypes; consult knowledgeable practitioners.

## Combat design hypotheses
- Begin with three distinct styles in prototype; exact three to be selected during design.
- Inputs: light/heavy attack, guard/deflect, evade, stance/technique switch, lock-on.
- Posture and stamina systems are hypotheses to playtest, not approved final rules.
- Technique blending should have clear transition constraints, resource costs and readable animation.
- Boss learning should be bounded and explainable, never secretly omniscient.
- Mastery may improve animation refinement and transitions, but never hide essential mechanics behind opaque grinding.

## Open decisions
- Weapons: unarmed focus, hybrid weapons, or separate weapon disciplines.
- Starting three styles, camera, combat speed, progression economy, multiplayer, platforms, art pipeline, and exact Unreal version.
- World scale and production budget are not yet fixed.
